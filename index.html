NEED TO KNOW?

Frequently Asked
Questions

What are the benefits of attending your yoga classes?

YogMalti's yoga and wellness practices focus on mindful movement, strength, flexibility, breathing, healthy habits and overall wellbeing.

Do I need prior yoga experience?

No. Our classes are suitable for different levels of practitioners, from beginners to experienced practitioners. Modifications and variations can be used to accommodate different skill levels and physical abilities.

What should I wear to your yoga classes?

Comfortable, breathable clothing that allows a full range of motion is ideal. Yoga pants or leggings and a loose-fitting top are suitable options.

What should I bring to class?

Bring a yoga mat, a water bottle and a towel. If you have any props such as blocks or straps, you can bring them as well.
